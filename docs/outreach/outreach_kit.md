**English version (for your reference, send the Albanian):**

Dear Minister Ibrahimaj / Dear Director,

I am Lorenc Hoxha, an Albanian who has lived in Ho Chi Minh City for nine years, where I run Plan A Production, a production company with 20 Vietnamese employees, clients such as Mirinda, Biti's and Vinasoy, and projects in six countries in the region.

I followed the July signing of the labour-mobility Memorandum with Vietnam and the plan for an Albania–Vietnam Economic and Employment Forum. From my experience here, three things will decide whether the agreement brings real workers within a year:

1. Choosing the licensed Vietnamese labour-export agencies. There are hundreds; a small share are serious. I know them first-hand and can help with a vetted list.
2. Pre-departure information. Vietnamese workers do not know where Albania is, what it pays, or what conditions to expect. Short materials in Vietnamese sharply reduce contract abandonment.
3. The Vietnamese side of the Forum: who is invited, who speaks to them, and how the message travels in Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City.

I am not asking for a contract or a position. I am offering my time and my network here, as an Albanian who wants this agreement to work. If useful, a 20-minute call with whoever runs the implementation plan would be enough to see where I can help concretely.

Respectfully,
Lorenc Hoxha
